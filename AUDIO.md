# Audio

All sound in Riverbound is **synthesised at runtime** with the Web Audio API. There are no audio files: every sound effect and all of the music are generated by the game's code.

## Engine (`src/audio/AudioEngine.js`)

- **One `AudioContext`.** It starts on the first click or key press, per the browser autoplay policy.
- **Mix buses:** music, ambience, effects and interface. Each is set from **Settings → Audio**. The master bus goes through a gentle compressor.
- **Mute when unfocused:** optional, on by default.
- **A shared reverb:** a convolver with a generated decaying-noise impulse response (3.2 s).
- **Noise buffers:** white, pink and brown loops generated once, with crossfaded seams.
- **Helpers:**
  - enveloped oscillator tones with pitch glides;
  - filtered noise bursts;
  - stereo voices with reverb sends;
  - looping filtered-noise beds.

## Ambience (`src/audio/AudioSystem.js`)

Continuous beds whose levels are driven every frame by the game state:

| Bed | Driven by |
|-|-|
| River | Current speed at the player's position (brown noise, low-pass) |
| Hull lapping | Boat speed. The filter opens as you row faster |
| Rapids | The rapids factor under the boat |
| Waterfalls | Distance to spawned waterfalls |
| Wind + gusts | Weather wind × the biome's wind character, storms. Gusts from two slow sines |
| Rain | Rain intensity (high hiss + on-water patter) |
| Sea swell | Sea / coast proximity, slow swell |
| Night shimmer | Night × the biome's insect level, reduced in rain |

Wildlife voices are procedural one-shots, scheduled by biome (`ambience` in `biomes.json`), time of day and weather:

- **Birds:** trills, two-note calls, rising whistles and chatter. There are more at dawn, fewer in rain.
- **Cicadas:** amplitude-modulated noise on hot afternoons.
- **Crickets:** at night.
- **Frogs:** filtered saw croaks, more in wetlands and rain. They are also triggered by the frog models near the player.
- **Owls:** rare, at night.
- **Heron:** calls when startled.
- **Village bells:** near settlements.

| Effect | Sound |

## Adaptive music (`src/audio/Music.js`)

The music is generative, sparse and quiet. It never loops the same way twice.

- **Key, mode and timbre come from the biome** (`music` in `biomes.json`). Blossom Vale is D major pentatonic on a harp-like pluck. The jungle is dorian on marimba. The misty highlands are aeolian on a breathy flute. Crystal Lakes and Frostreach are lydian on bells.
- **Time of day:** night slows the tempo, darkens the pad filter, lowers the key, and shifts major keys toward lydian. Golden hour and dawn brighten it and add a little density.
- **Weather:** storms switch to minor modes and darker filters. Rain softens.
- **Structure:**
  - sections of 70–130 s, separated by rests of 35–105 s;
  - within a section: slow pad chords that walk a progression around the tonic, a soft root, and a melody that wanders mostly stepwise with occasional octave echoes.
- **Moments:** gameplay events (`music:moment`) trigger short stingers in the current key:
  - a rising arpeggio for a discovery;
  - a swelling chord with bells for wonders and lit lamps;
  - two bells for a completed request;
  - a new section cued on entering a new land or chapter.

`tests/unit/systems.test.mjs` checks that the musical context responds to time and weather and that generated chords stay in scale. `tests/e2e/features.mjs` checks that the context runs, the beds are audible, the master output carries signal, and a music section plays.

## Accessibility

When **Subtitles** are on, important sounds are captioned:

- thunder (near or distant);
- a lamp flaring;
- the roar of a waterfall;
- owl calls;
- village bells.

Captions follow the subtitle size setting. Dialogue is always shown as text.
